Daily news

November 10, 2026

Muse Ember, the smallest and cheapest Muse model

Meta announced November 9 that Muse Ember is live in Muse Code and on the Meta Model API as muse-ember-1.0. It is built for the repetitive work agents do all day, with a 1M-token context window and up to 512K output tokens, priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.004 per million.

November 10, 2026

Reported: Meta lists rupee prices for Muse ahead of an India launch

Meta has listed rupee prices ahead of an India launch: Power at ₹1,799 and Maximum at ₹8,999, close to the US $20 and $100 monthly plans. Indian users are still on a waitlist and the app is not in Indian app stores yet. Meta has not confirmed a launch date, whether these prices are final, or whether they are monthly.

November 9, 2026

Cloudflare: websites foot the bill for AI agents, and Muse is the busiest of them

Cloudflare argued November 5 that AI agents acting for people have cut the link between a website and the person behind it, leaving the site to pay when an order is disputed or an article is read without a visit. It cites HUMAN's finding that Meta's Muse accounted for 72% of AI agent requests.

November 7, 2026

Muse Spaces: describe an idea, get a custom app

Meta said November 6 that Muse can now build a space from a plain description — an order tracker, a client hub, a gift tracker. Spaces sit in the sidebar and stay current from the apps you connect, and they are available everywhere Muse is available, starting the same day.

November 7, 2026

Muse's models now remember better and recover when a task goes wrong

Meta AI Research said November 6 that Muse and Meta AI now run on specialized versions of their existing models, trained using Muse Wonder. Active Memory is meant to remember what matters across conversations; Active Focus keeps working toward a goal when something unexpected gets in the way. Meta reports a 2.4x reduction in forgetting critical information, on its own tests.

November 6, 2026

Muse Voice, speech-first

Meta said November 5 that real-time voice and expressive avatars are rolling out in the Muse app in the US, on mobile, web and Mac. Talk instead of typing, interrupt as you go, and the avatar reacts as you speak. Voice on AI Glasses and Muse Charm is coming soon, with no date given.

Field discoveries

  • Thumbnail-first: a thumbnail decides in the first 3 seconds. Test it before you ship anything visual.
  • Test demand before you pick a niche: look for a person, not a niche — someone with a problem worth paying to solve.
  • Render it before you build it: use AI to mock up the idea and show it to real people before you spend weeks building it.
  • Stop at a confirmed block: if a platform confirms you are blocked, stop. No bypass, no retry loop, no scraper.
  • Low competition doesn't mean no money: a quiet niche can still pay — check whether anyone in it is actually spending.
  • Muse is free to start: invite code GZTOI6, mentioned in the discoveries track. The code is also on the home page.
