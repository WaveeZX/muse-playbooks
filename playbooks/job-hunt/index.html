Job Hunt — Any Work Type

Works for remote, hybrid and onsite roles. The method is the same: define the job you can accept before you apply to anything.

The short versionSet your floor first — minimum pay, work type, location or time zone, schedule, and benefits. Then search to that floor, verify every listing at the source, tailor honestly, compare total pay, and check every application before you send it.

The 7 steps

  1. Set your floor firstMinimum pay, work type, location or time zone, schedule, and benefits you need. Write it down before you look at a single listing.
  2. Search to your floor, not to a vibeRank roles against your actual experience and proof — a portfolio and measurable results beat claims.
  3. Verify on the employer's own pageListings get copied and go stale. Confirm title, pay range, work type and requirements at the source, before you spend time on it.
  4. Tailor honestlyMatch your real work to the posting's language. Reframe, never invent.
  5. Compare total payBase + employer retirement match + health costs + PTO + schedule. A higher headline salary can pay less.
  6. QC before submit — every timeName, contact, work history, education, salary, and the exact job title and company. Never guess a salary or a date.
  7. Track everythingCompany, role, date, status, next step. Check for a prior application before applying again.

QC check

Would every line on this application survive the employer reading it back to you in an interview? If not, fix it or cut it.

Kill ruleIf a portal forces an account with no guest, Google or LinkedIn sign-in option and the role isn't a strong match at strong pay, skip it. Your hours are worth more than a broken funnel.

Deep dive: templates & the meat

Application tracker fields (one row per application)

Company · role · source · date applied · pay range · work type · status · next step · date of next step. Before applying anywhere, search this tracker and your email for the company — double applications look careless.

Total-pay worksheet

Base pay + employer retirement match (check vesting and plan-year rules) − health premium difference − surcharges + PTO value + schedule value. Compare a full year on both sides, not the offer letter's first line.

Screening answers

Write your standing answers down once (authorization, sponsorship, location, clearance, relationships) and reuse them verbatim. If a question is not covered, stop and find out — a guessed answer can end a candidacy or worse. Also agree your reason for leaving and earliest start date with yourself in advance, so every application tells the same true story.
